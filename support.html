Subscriptions

Premium is an auto-renewing subscription bought through Apple. To cancel or change it, open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions. You can also open Manage subscription inside the app. To get Premium back on a new phone, tap Restore purchases on the Premium screen.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple, so refunds are requested at reportaproblem.apple.com.
