Privacy Policy

Santé: Party Games

The short version

Santé doesn’t collect your name or any personal details. There is no account, no tracking, no analytics and no advertising. The only data involved is your subscription, which is handled as described under Purchases.

What you enter

Player names, genders and sexualities are only kept in the phone’s memory while the app is open. They are used to give the right turn to the right person and to pair people in flirty dares, and they are never shown, saved or sent anywhere. They are erased when you close the app.

What is saved

The only thing saved on your phone is the language you picked and, in test builds, a pretend subscription.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple, together with RevenueCat, a service that keeps track of subscriptions for us. RevenueCat sees an anonymous ID and what you bought, never your name, Apple ID or payment details. Apple’s and RevenueCat’s privacy policies apply to purchases.

No internet needed

The games work without an internet connection. The app only goes online to buy or restore a subscription and to check that it is still active. We don’t have a server of our own.

Adults only

Santé is meant for adults aged 18 and over and is not aimed at children.
